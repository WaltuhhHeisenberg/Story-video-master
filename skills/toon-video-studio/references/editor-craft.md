# Editing craft for the 3D render (execution, not art direction)

## Principles
- **The scene map is the user's.** The 3D build applies it faithfully; findings are flagged, never silently changed.
- **A visible change at least every 4 seconds.** Every clause (3-4 s) has its own camera shot, so the picture changes by construction; a longer clause gets a mid-clause caption.
- **Motion is motivated.** Slow push-in/pull-back by default; whip (fast orbit) only where the map says `whip_pan`; no two identical set-ups more than 4 in a row.
- **Respect the voice.** The voiceover is muxed as delivered. A pause of 0.6 s or more is a held moment: the actor stands idle, the camera keeps its slow move.
- **Captions carry weight, once per clause**, short (every new number, date and name from the map), burned in at the lower third, legible at phone size.
- **Consistency.** A story section is one world (same theme, same seed); returning to a place reuses its seed.

## The five review passes (run in order, report only)
1. **Timing and continuity**: shots = clauses, times on the frame grid, scenes cover the voiceover exactly (G1-01, G1-02).
2. **Rhythm and density**: longest shot <= 4.5 s, repeats <= 4 (G1-04, G1-06); no stretch of equally intense camera moves.
3. **Themes and actions**: each section's theme fits its mood words; actions fit the literal (plan table in `scene_map_bridge_report.md`).
4. **Captions**: count = overlay_text count, wording exactly as in the map, inside their scene (G1-07, G2-05).
5. **Technical**: Gate 2 plus the contact sheet (blank frames, change across every cut, duration, size, audio).
Unsure about a treatment? Choose the quieter one and list the alternative in the report.
